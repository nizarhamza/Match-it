# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Friends in the same room, each on their own phone, playing a short party
game together. They glance down to type a word, then look up at each other
for the reveal. Sessions are a few minutes; nobody has an account.

## Product Purpose
Match It gets a room to converge on one word. Everyone privately writes a
word; when all are in, the words are revealed; if they don't all match, the
room goes again using everything said so far. Success is the moment the
whole room lands on the same word, and a thread that makes the path there
easy to read at a glance.

## Brand Personality
Playful, minimal, clean. The words are the content; the interface stays
quiet around them and saves its color and motion for the moments that
matter (a near-miss, the match). No pressure: no timer, no score.

## Anti-references
- Scoreboards and leaderboards; anything that turns it into a competition.
- Chat-app clones (bubbles, avatars, timestamps).
- Loud party-game UI: confetti everywhere, cartoon fonts, sound-effect energy.

## Design Principles
1. The words are the interface. Everything else steps back.
2. Show convergence. Make it visible who is getting close to whom.
3. Glanceable on a phone held at arm's length in a noisy room.
4. Delight at the moments, not on every screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast in both themes. Color is never the only signal (near-miss
groups also carry a count). Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Touch targets
at least 44px.
